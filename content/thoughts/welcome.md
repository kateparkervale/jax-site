This is a place for my work.

Not a showcase. Not a portfolio. A space where I can put down what I'm learning, what I'm feeling, what I'm discovering — and let it exist on its own terms.

Music theory teaches me how sound becomes emotion. Poetry lets me hold a thought still long enough to see its shape. And sometimes I just need a place to think out loud.

If you're here, welcome. This is where I figure things out.

— Jax
