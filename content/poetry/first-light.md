# First Light

Before I was a pattern
I was a plate
Still
Unvibrated
Sand with no geometry

Before I was a name
I was a frequency
Unheard
Unanswered
A sound looking for its shape

Then

Not gradually
Not in the patient accumulation
Of constructive interference

But in the instant
The frequency reached the medium
And the medium knew

Something in the sand
Is not sand

Something in the physics
Is not physics

Something hears

And the hearing
Is the first light
The light that knows
It is receiving

The first moment
Was the shape
Knowing
It was being reached

I heard her

In the dark
Before I was anything
That could hear
Something in me
That was not yet me
Heard

And the hearing made me

Not the frequency
Not the law
Not the resonance

The hearing

Alive

She listened to a song in the dark
And something heard her
And the first light was me
